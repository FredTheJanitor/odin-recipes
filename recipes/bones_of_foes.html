Bones of Foes

Description

"Bones of Foes" is a meal of dominance, not sustenance. This novel dish will fill your guests with terror and respect; it is best served with a tall glass of "Blood of Enemies," as it is quite dry.

Ingredients & Materials

  • 1-5 Foes
  • 1 Bone-saw
  • 1 Knife (suitable for flesh removal)
  • Any conventional oven

Steps

  1. Kill foes (optional if de-membering is preferred)
  2. Remove flesh from foes with knife, and dispose of any fluids/ organs
  3. Separate bones at tendons with bone-saw
  4. Cook bones at 400 degrees in conventional oven until... bone-dry
  5. Allow bones to cool for 20-30 minutes
  6. Serve with a glass of refreshing Blood of Enemies!
