Blood of Enemies

Description

"Blood of Enemies" is a drink that is more of a statement of power than taste. This unique beverage is sure to instil fear in those that stand against you; it is best served alone, as there will be only one person left to drink from its cup.

Ingredients & Materials

  • 1-5 Enemies
  • 1 Vacutainer blood-collection system per enemy
  • 1 Cup (preferably a wine glass)
  • Optional: tears of enemies' loved ones

Steps

  1. Slay enemies (best served fresh, no later than 1 day after death)
  2. Insert Vacutainer entry-tube into vein or artery of victim
  3. Insert Vacutainer exit-tube into cup
  4. Optional: Garnish with tears of enemies' loved ones to taste
  5. Enjoy!
