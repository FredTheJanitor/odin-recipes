Flesh of Adversaries

Description

"Flesh of Adversaries" is a dish of dominion and flavor. This succulant roast is sure to scare and satisfy; it is a versatile meal that works well in many settings that require pronounced social hierarchy.

Ingredients & Materials

  • 1-5 Adversaries
  • 1 Knife
  • 1 Conventional oven
  • Spices (many appropriate options)

Steps

  1. Extinguish adversary (best served immediately from panoply to plate)
  2. With knife separate flesh from adversary
  3. Separate cuts of meat to preferance
  4. Add spices
  5. Roast in oven at 325 until internal temperature reaches 145
  6. Can be served in many forms such as sandwiches, meat pies, or any method from Dahmer's cook-book
